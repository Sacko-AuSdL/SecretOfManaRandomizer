# Alpha 0.1 — unofficial Open World playtest (RC2)

An unofficial experimental fork of
[Moppleton's Secret of Mana Randomizer](https://github.com/Moppu/SecretOfManaRandomizer),
not an official upstream or stable release. We're inviting volunteers for full
Open World runs.

Highlights: Sandship boss/reward content; conservative special-boss integrations;
Mixed/Weapon Only Crystal Orbs; Per Character/Party Spell Shuffle with destination
spirit affinity; Cannon Open/Shuffle/Chaos; free fares, Kakkara and Orb info;
reduced forging, optional player magic scaling, cosmetic Manabite and save/load
music restoration. Weapon Power and direct-arena prototypes are excluded.

Extract the complete ZIP on Windows. Requires .NET Framework 4.6.2 or a compatible
later 4.x runtime and your own unmodified US Secret of Mana v1.0 ROM (2 MiB,
optionally a 512-byte copier header). No ROM/music pack included. Start with
StarterSettings/Short.options.txt and seed Alpha01-RC1-Short; retained RC1 seed
labels make RC1/RC2 comparisons deterministic.

Known limitations: occasional defeated-shadow ghost visual with three Sprite
roles, conservative arena compatibility, and no guaranteed save compatibility
across versions. Other modes are outside this playtest's acceptance scope.
The app displays upstream version 1.49; report Alpha 0.1 RC2 and the EXE hash.
The build is unsigned. Full playthrough coverage is still being gathered.

Report seed, complete settings, emulator/version and exact reproduction steps
through this fork's Issues. Back up saves, preserve pre-failure saves, redact
personal paths in logs and never upload ROMs.

Thanks to Moppleton and upstream contributors. Manabite artwork/palette by
**Tomm (Discord)**, with creator use/redistribution permission confirmed by the
maintainer. Source, LICENSE and third-party notices are included.
